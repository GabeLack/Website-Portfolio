AI for automatic RF Mapping from a video

Project Link

This was a project I did with a classmate during an internship at Linköping University. The lab wanted a piece of software that could process a video with simple painted dots on human skin and a bending filament to with dual measurements of nerve reactions to create an RF Map. We used about 30 videos to train a model on DeepLabCut with additional imputation to process videos in similar requirements.
